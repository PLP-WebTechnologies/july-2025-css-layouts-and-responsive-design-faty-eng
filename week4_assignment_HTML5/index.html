🏜️ About Marrakesh

Marrakesh is a vibrant city in Morocco known for its colorful souks, historic medina, and the stunning Atlas Mountains in the distance. It's a city full of life, history, and culture.

📸 Must-See Places

🕌 Koutoubia Mosque
🌴 Majorelle Garden
🛍️ Medina Souks
🐫 Desert Excursions

🧕 Moroccan Culture

Moroccan culture is a blend of Arab, Berber, and French influences. Enjoy traditional dishes like couscous 🍲 and tagine 🍛, and experience music, crafts, and vibrant street life.
